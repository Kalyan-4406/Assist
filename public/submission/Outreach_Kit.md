# Campus Build Race — proposed outreach kit

Simulation only. No messages were sent. Use the public app to generate current campus-specific referral links and labelled counts. Obtain group-admin permission. Budget rewards are proposals; do not promise them.

Public asset: https://campus-build-race-nxtwave.vabjashwanthreddy.chatgpt.site

## Day 1: ambassador recruitment

English: “We’re planning a free online workshop to build a first AI project in one hour. Would you help your campus by sharing its page in one relevant class or coding-club group, with permission? Students see a Build Card before registering. Date to be announced. Choose your campus at the link above.”

Hinglish: “Ek free online workshop plan ho raha hai: ek ghante mein apna first AI project. Apne campus ka page ek relevant class ya coding-club group mein permission ke saath share karoge? Register karne se pehle Build Card milta hai. Date abhi announce honi hai. Upar ke link par apna campus choose karo.”

Operator workflow: register normally, privately mark the approved representative in /ops, and give them their /me/code share kit. Set an initial goal of 130 unique relevant students reached. Track attributed joins rather than claiming reach from group size. Recruit 25 active representatives across at least 15 campuses.

## Day 4: recovery gate

If real cumulative joins are below 240, inspect views → starts → cards → submits. Low views: add active representatives and relevant groups. High views but low starts: test R (resume), P (placement), F (friends) pitches. Form drop-off: review the mobile path and connectivity. Use the labelled selected dataset in Morning Brief; do not claim demo records are real acquisitions.

## Day 7: final campaign-day message

“Our seven-day registration campaign is ending. The workshop date is still to be announced. If a first AI project would be useful, choose your Build Card and register for details. Share only with classmates who would benefit.”

Only send this on the genuine last day of the campaign. Do not claim seats are running out or registration is permanently closing. Use real verified dates when available.

## Readiness reminder — draft only

“Before the workshop, charge your laptop, create a free AI account and prepare an empty GitHub repository. Tick these on the confirmation page in the browser where you registered. Workshop date: to be announced.”

The app records consent and prepares copy; it does not send email or WhatsApp messages. An organiser must confirm the event, delivery provider and recipient consent before activating reminders.

## Three interview questions

1. What stops you from beginning an AI project you could explain in an interview?
2. When a workshop link appears in a class group, what makes you tap it or ignore it?
3. After registering for a free workshop, what usually helps you actually attend?

Interviews were not conducted. Record actual anonymised responses and permission before adding a quote to the deck; never present these prompts or the brief’s assumptions as student testimony.

## Five-person phone test protocol

Give five final-year students the public campus link on their own phones using mobile data. Ask them to register without hints, then find their referral link and readiness checklist. Obtain permission before storing real details. Time from first Claim tap to confirmation; record branch/interest taps, stalls, errors, horizontal overflow and whether demo labels are understood. Target median under 45 seconds. Delete QA records only through an authorised operator workflow.

Results: not conducted. Automated 360px browser tests are documented separately and do not substitute for these five participants.

## Approval and spend controls

Proposed ceiling: ₹900 campus prizes + ₹500 referral rewards + ₹400 boost + ₹200 reserve = ₹2,000. Confirm rules with the organiser before announcing any benefit. Track receipts and cumulative spend; stop at the cap. Release boost/reserve only after real conversion indicates a working channel.
