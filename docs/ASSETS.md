# Generated visual assets

Tool: built-in Image Gen. Original PNGs and concept mockups remain in the complete local submission package and are excluded from this lean repository. Real product navigation, fields, content and controls are HTML/React; the generated scene supplies imagery only.

Prompt briefs used in this session (summarized; the original tool prompts are in the conversation):

1. **Home concept:** Redesign Campus Build Race for final-year engineering students. Hyper-realistic dark workspace imagery against an airy white/cobalt product UI. Include the whole landing page: existing navigation/demo notice, offer and Find your college / Explore the race actions, finder plus live college race, a three-stage workshop journey and a photo footer. Keep the working app's information architecture and do not invent acquired students, dates, perks, quotes or claims.
2. **Campus concept:** Matching whole college page with a photographic workspace, native campus proof/count/rank/branch breakdown, a dimensional blue Build Passport, Claim my seat, workshop outcomes and race/50-registration goal. Demonstration metrics are illustrative and must be labelled in implementation.
3. **Mobile/state concept:** Matching phone-sized campus view and registration sheet. Preserve three steps, reward before contact information, name/email fields, explicit consent and a reachable primary action. Use the same palette and realistic workspace motif.
4. **Standalone workspace asset:** Hyper-realistic silver laptop displaying a coding interface on a dark wooden desk, cobalt blue metal race flag branded “Campus Build Race”, dark notebook and mug, soft daylight and an empty dark left side for native HTML text. No people or invented student/social proof. Branding on the physical flag belongs in the asset; page text and buttons remain code-native.

Production assets: `public/images/build-workspace.webp` (1600×900), `build-workspace-1200.webp` (1200px wide), and `build-workspace-800.webp` (800px wide). These images are generated illustrations, not photos documenting a real event.

The complete local submission package contains the fidelity ledger and visual comparison evidence.
